# 0020. Logo: a leather satchel with an envelope flap

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-24

## Context

The first icon was a generic blue briefcase. At small sizes it read as a
padlock or a shopping bag, and nothing in it said mail.

## Decision

The logo is a classic satchel whose flap comes to a point like the back of an
envelope, fastened with a round clasp, with two tone-on-tone straps. At large
sizes it also has stitching round the body (no stitching on the straps).
It sits on a leather-tan tile (`#d4925a` → `#b06f3c`, bag `#fffaf4`, flap
`#f3dcc4`, lines `#9c5a28`).

Rejected along the way: an envelope sticking out of the bag (crowds the
satchel), a stamp and address on the flap (too busy), buckled straps (compete
with the V), and dropping the clasp (looks like a stock mail icon).

`scripts/logo.py` draws the logo and renders every icon, so change the shapes
there rather than editing the files it writes:

- `apps/desktop/icons/icon.svg`: the detailed app icon on the macOS 824/1024 grid.
- `apps/desktop/icons/icon-small.svg`: the simplified drawing (no
  stitching or clasp dot, heavier handle) used at 32px and below.
- `ui/public/favicon.svg`: the simplified drawing, full bleed, for the
  browser tab and the sidebar.
- `docs/brand/satchel-mark.svg`: the bag alone on a transparent background.

The PNGs, `icon.icns` and `icon.ico` are rendered from these with
`tauri icon`, with the 16–32px slots taken from the simplified drawing.

## Consequences

- The UI accent follows the logo instead of the old blue: `#9c5a28` in light
  mode (5.4:1 on white) and `#d4925a` in dark mode (6.5:1 on the panel), with
  outgoing chat bubbles warmed to match. The warning amber (`--warn`) is now
  close in hue to the accent, so warnings lean on their icons and wording.
- Small sizes need their own drawing; regenerate both when the shape changes.
