Free to use, not free to run.

satchel.email has no ads, no subscription and no server holding your mail, so nothing pays for it except people who want it to keep going. If it's useful to you, you can sponsor it on GitHub.

Sponsor on GitHub

What your sponsorship supports

satchel.email is one of several open-source projects by the same person, David Figatner. A GitHub sponsorship goes to me, not to one project, so it supports all of them:

satchel.email

This app: email, calendar and contacts that keep your mail on your own computer.

Golem Docs

One editor for every kind of document (Word, PDF, Markdown, drawings) that keeps them as ordinary files in a folder.

pixi-viewport

A 2D camera for pixi.js with drag, pinch, zoom and follow. Now looked after by the pixi.js community.

intersects

Collision tests for 2D shapes: points, circles, ellipses, lines, boxes and polygons.

pixi.js helpers

pixi-scrollbox, pixi-ease, pixi-cull and pixi-dashed-line.

Vanilla UI pieces

window-manager, tree, shape-points and many smaller libraries.

Where the money goes

Sponsorships pay the costs above first. Anything beyond them pays for time spent on these projects. GitHub takes no fee on sponsorships from personal accounts.

These projects are maintained by one person, not a charity, so sponsorships aren't tax-deductible.
